# 0005. All nine targets are kept unchanged after the EDA; two scoring rules are fixed now

Status: accepted
Decided-by: answerer (inferred)
Question: T-007 (03-eda), milestone 4 gate: on EDA evidence alone, should any target in the brief's "Goals and success metrics" table be revised, especially lowest per-class recall (CW/TD/INJ load-coverage gaps) and the physics check (AF exhaust-temperature mechanism failed; CW signature is variance, not level)?
Decision: No target value or stretch value changes. The single allowed revision window closes with this ADR, and the targets are final. The metric definitions are made precise before any model is trained, without changing any number:
1. Lowest per-class recall is taken over the five fault classes on pooled leave-one-load-out predictions. Normal is already bound by the false alarm rate. Per-fold recall is reported with its row count. A fold with no rows of a class shows "n/a", not 0. INJ at 75% (61 rows) is reported but flagged as high-variance.
2. Physics check: the reference is `reports/engineering_checklist.md` as committed at the end of the EDA. That means each fault's "Expected top 5" plus the channels marked "Agree" in "Observed". A fault matches if at least 3 of its top-5 mean-|SHAP| features map to those channels. Derived features (residual, rolling mean or std, cylinder spread, ratio) count as the channels they are built from. A second condition is that no top-5 feature is a day-dependent channel from "Channels that must not drive a diagnosis" (T12, T18, T21, Pl_water2).
Basis: The brief says targets "can be revised once, after the EDA, with the reason written down", and ranks honest evaluation first (section 10). The coverage gaps are not new evidence. The brief's Validation protocol already says "The 75% fold has no cavitation or turbine runs, so per-fold scores cover only the classes present" and "pool the predictions for the headline metrics". Pooled, every fault has at least 6,492 rows (01_coverage.csv). Every fault shows a strong, physically grounded signature: AC T15 +29.8 SD, TD matches the whole exhaust chain, AF Pturb −1.62 SD, INJ a 2.5 SD cylinder spread, and CW a rolling-std jump at switch-on. So no target looks unreachable, and none can be raised without model evidence. CW's variance signature was pre-registered: its Expected top 5 already names the rolling std of Pl_water1 and Qw_eng. AF's failed mechanism is exactly the one miss that "4 of 5 faults" allows. AF also leads with Pturb, which agrees with the data. The AF delay in the first minutes after switch-on is consistent with a 10-min median delay. Brief line 331: "The checklist written at the end of the EDA is compared against each fault's top 5 SHAP features."
Consequences: `reports/targets.md` states that every target is kept, cites this ADR and ends with "Targets final as of 2026-09-28." Roadmap item 8 scores the physics check with rule 2 exactly. The evaluation reports per-fold recall with n. Feature engineering has to include rolling-std features, or the CW physics check cannot pass. No target may change after this point.
