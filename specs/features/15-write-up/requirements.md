# 15 Write-up: requirements

- R1. The README states the results as locked (the nine targets, result against target, met or not), what they mean in engineering terms, how the models were tested, how to run the demo and how to reproduce everything, the dataset citation (both works) and the limits.
- R2. A paper in the owner's preprint template (`paper/preprint.sty`, the form of the owner's `main.tex` of 29 September 2026): title between rules, abstract, contributions, TikZ diagrams, pgfplots figures from CSV, tables with booktabs, bold-lead captions, verification, limitations, conclusion, natbib references.
- R3. Every number, table and plot in the paper is generated by `paper/make_data.py` from the locked results files; a test fails if the committed copies drift.
- R4. `scripts/reproduce.sh` rebuilds every experiment, notebook, figure and demo file from a fresh clone, in the order they were made, skipping work whose output exists.
- R5. Drafts for the owner to publish: a LinkedIn post and the Marine AIMS README link (`docs/launch/`). Nothing is published by the agent.
- R6. Anything the write-up exposes that contradicts the locked evaluation is fixed where it is shown (demo, card), recorded in an ADR, and guarded by a test; the locked scores themselves never change.
